## Falling Meteors for Forge 1.20.1

The first release of Falling Meteors for modern Minecraft, continued by **blueDev** from the original 1.7.10 mod by **AlexDGr8r**.

### Requirements
- Minecraft **1.20.1**
- Forge **47** or newer
- Install on the server and on every client

### Install
Download `meteors-forge-1.20.1-2.15.0.jar` below and put it in your `mods` folder.

### What's in it
Everything from the original mod:
- The 5 meteor types, plus comets and the Comet Kitty shower
- Craters with hot ore, and the Kreknorite Nether portal shrine
- The Meteor Shield, Protected Land Tester, Meteor Timer and the 3 detectors
- The Freezer and Slippery Blocks
- Meteor Summoners
- Meteorite, Frezarite and Kreknorite tools and armor with their abilities
- Alien Creepers and Comet Kitties
- Commands, game rules, advancements, ore generation and 8 languages
